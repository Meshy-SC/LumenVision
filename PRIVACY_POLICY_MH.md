# LumenVision Privacy Policy — Meta Quest

- **App:** LumenVision (on the Meta Horizon Store)
- **Publisher:** Meshy (Ahmed Dalhi)
- **Last updated:** November 26, 2026

---

## Summary

LumenVision is a paid game on the Meta Horizon Store. It has no in-app purchases, no ads and no accounts of its own. **LumenVision collects and processes the following data:**

- an **ownership check** with Meta when the app starts, so that the app knows your copy came from the Meta Horizon Store;
- your **pointer input** (where you point and select on the game's window), while you play;
- your **game progress and settings**, which are stored on your headset;
- **crash reports**, if Meta Horizon OS sends one to Meta;
- any **email you send us**.

The only Meta Horizon platform service LumenVision uses is the ownership check.

Any user can ask us to delete their data at any time, free of charge. Section 7 explains how.

## 2. Data We Process and How We Use It

### 2.1 Ownership check (entitlement)

When the app starts, it asks Meta Horizon OS, through the Meta Horizon Platform SDK, whether your Meta account is entitled to LumenVision. Meta answers yes or no. The app uses the answer only for this check, and keeps it in memory only while it runs. Meta processes this request for your Meta account under [Meta's Privacy Policy](https://www.meta.com/legal/privacy-policy/).

### 2.2 Controllers and hands

LumenVision is a window (a "panel") in your headset. Meta Horizon OS turns your controller or hand pointer into taps on that window, as on a touch screen: the app receives where on its window you point and select, so that you can steer the marble and press buttons. This input is used only while you play, in memory.

### 2.3 Emails you send us

If you email us for support or to request deletion, we receive your email address, your message, and anything you include. We use it only to answer you or to handle your request. Your email passes through our email provider. We keep it only while we handle your request. For deletion requests, we delete your email once we have confirmed the deletion to you.

---

## 3. Data Stored on Your Headset

To keep your game going, the app stores the following in its private storage on your headset:

- Your best score, best distance and best combo
- Your coin balance (coins are earned only by playing and cannot be bought), the marble skins you unlocked with coins and the skin you selected
- The day you last claimed the daily coin reward
- Whether you have seen the first-run tutorial
- Your settings: music and sound volume, sound on or off, Reduce Motion and High Contrast

**Meta cloud backup.** If cloud backup is turned on in your headset's settings, Meta Horizon OS may include this data in the backup Meta keeps for your Meta account, so that it can be restored when you install LumenVision again, on the same headset or another one. Meta runs that backup under [Meta's Privacy Policy](https://www.meta.com/legal/privacy-policy/).

Progress on LumenVision for Apple devices is separate: nothing is shared between the two.

---

## 4. Crash Reports

If the app crashes, Meta Horizon OS may report it to Meta, and Meta's developer dashboard then shows us the technical details: the headset model, the Meta Horizon OS and app versions, and where in the code the crash happened. We use them only to fix the problem.

## 5. Where Your Data Is Stored and Who Can Access It

- **Your game progress and settings** (section 3) are stored on your headset, and in Meta's cloud backup if you have it turned on.
- **The ownership check** is handled by **Meta** as part of your Meta account.
- LumenVision has no servers of its own. The developer can see, in Meta's developer dashboard, the sales of the app and the crash details described in section 4.

## 7. Requesting Data Deletion

**All users, in every country, can ask us to delete their data at any time. It is free, and you do not have to give a reason.**

### Data on your headset

You can delete it yourself, immediately: **Settings → Reset All Progress** deletes your scores, coins and unlocked skins, and **uninstalling LumenVision** permanently deletes all of the app's data from your headset. If cloud backup is on, turn it off for LumenVision in your headset's settings first, or the backup can restore it when you install the app again.

### Any other data

Email **davinci.dalhi@gmail.com** with the subject **"Data deletion request"**, from any email address, and tell us which data you want deleted. Within **30 days** we delete all data we hold about you, including any emails you have sent us, guide you through deleting the data stored on your headset and in Meta's cloud backup, and confirm the deletion to you by email.

### Data held by Meta

Your Meta account, your purchase of LumenVision and Meta's cloud backups are managed by Meta. You can view, download or delete them with Meta's own tools in your Meta account settings ([Meta's Privacy Center](https://www.meta.com/privacy/)).
