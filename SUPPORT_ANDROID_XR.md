# LumenVision Support — Android XR

LumenVision is a one-tap arcade climbing game for Android XR headsets, published by Meshy on Google Play.
Contact: **davinci.dalhi@gmail.com** — we answer within 48 hours.

## Playing

- **The goal:** a glowing marble climbs a shaft on its own. Climb as high as you can.
- **Steer:** look or point at the game's window and pinch. Each pinch flips the marble's sideways direction. It also bounces off the walls of the shaft.
- **Gates:** every gate is sealed by two machines (saw blades, crushers, grinders, pincers, arc emitters and turbines) with one gap between them. Thread the gap; touching a machine costs a life.
- **Coins and combos:** each gap holds a coin. Grab coins in quick succession to raise your combo multiplier (up to x8). Threading a gap right through its centre scores a PERFECT bonus.
- **Faster and harder:** every 120 points is a new level. The climb speeds up, gaps narrow and more gates slide from side to side. The challenge comes in waves, with calmer stretches in between.
- **Lives and checkpoints:** you start with 3 lives and can bank up to 5 (bonus lives at 500, 1,500, 3,000, 5,000 and 8,000 points). Every 10th gate is a checkpoint. After losing a life you come back just above your last checkpoint, with a shield and a clear stretch of shaft ahead before the next gate.
- **Pause:** the pause button at the top left, or the system Back gesture. Leaving the window also pauses the run. From the pause screen you can return to the menu (the run is then not counted).
- The first time you play, the climb waits for your first pinch.

## Coins and skins

- Coins you collect are added to your wallet when a run ends. Once a day the menu offers a daily reward of 25 coins.
- Spend coins on the menu to unlock 10 marble skins, from Halo (40 coins) to Vesper (500 coins). Select a skin to wear it.
- Coins exist only inside the game. They cannot be bought, and LumenVision has no in-app purchases.

## Settings

Music and sound effect volume, Reduce Motion (calms idle animations and effects), High Contrast (bright edges on the machines), Send Feedback, Support, Privacy Policy, Reset All Progress, version and credits. The speaker button on the menu turns all sound on or off.

## Your progress

- Your progress is saved on your headset. If backup is on in your headset's settings, Android backs it up to your Google account, and it comes back when you install LumenVision again.
- **Settings → Reset All Progress** clears scores, coins and unlocked skins. Settings stay.
- Progress on other platforms stays there. Nothing transfers between platforms.
- For a refund, follow Google Play's refund policy (play.google.com, Account › Order history).
