# LumenVision Privacy Policy — Android XR

- **App:** LumenVision (on Google Play, for Android XR headsets)
- **Publisher:** Meshy (Ahmed Dalhi)
- **Last updated:** November 26, 2026

---

## Summary

LumenVision is a paid game on Google Play. It has no in-app purchases, no ads, no accounts of its own, and it does not use the internet: the app has no network permission at all. **LumenVision does not collect or share any personal data.** It processes:

- your **hand and eye input** (where you look or point and pinch on the game's window), while you play, on your headset only;
- your **game progress and settings**, which are stored on your headset;
- any **email you send us**.

Google Play handles your purchase of LumenVision, and Android may back up your progress to your Google account (section 3). Any user can ask us to delete their data at any time, free of charge. Section 7 explains how.

## 2. Data We Process and How We Use It

### 2.1 Hands and eyes

LumenVision is a window in your space. Android XR turns where you look or point, and your pinch, into taps on that window, as on a touch screen: the app receives only where on its window you pinch, so that you can steer the marble and press buttons. The app never receives camera images, eye images or hand-tracking data. This input is used only while you play, in memory, on your headset.

### 2.2 Your purchase

You buy LumenVision from Google Play. Google processes your payment and your Google account under [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy); we never receive your payment details. Google Play reports sales to us as totals and per-order records without your payment details, as it does for every developer.

### 2.3 Emails you send us

If you email us for support or to request deletion, we receive your email address, your message, and anything you include. We use it only to answer you or to handle your request. Your email passes through our email provider. We keep it only while we handle your request. For deletion requests, we delete your email once we have confirmed the deletion to you.

---

## 3. Data Stored on Your Headset

To keep your game going, the app stores the following in its private storage on your headset:

- Your best score, best distance and best combo
- Your coin balance (coins are earned only by playing and cannot be bought), the marble skins you unlocked with coins and the skin you selected
- The day you last claimed the daily coin reward
- Whether you have seen the first-run tutorial
- Your settings: music and sound volume, sound on or off, Reduce Motion and High Contrast

**Android backup.** If backup is turned on in your headset's settings, Android may include this file in the backup Google keeps for your Google account, so that it can be restored when you install LumenVision again. Only this save file is included. Google runs that backup under [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy); we cannot see it.

Progress on LumenVision for other platforms is separate: nothing is shared between them.

---

## 4. Crash Reports

The app contains no crash-reporting or analytics code. If you allow your headset to share usage and diagnostics with Google, Android may send crash reports to Google, and Google Play Console then shows us the technical details: the device model, the Android and app versions, and where in the code the crash happened. We use them only to fix the problem.

---

## 5. Where Your Data Is Stored and Who Can Access It

- **Your game progress and settings** (section 3) are stored on your headset, and in Android's backup of your Google account if you have it turned on.
- **Your purchase** is handled by **Google** as part of your Google account.
- LumenVision has no servers of its own and sends nothing to us. The developer can see, in Google Play Console, the sales of the app and the crash details described in section 4.
- No data processors, service providers or partners other than Google have access to this data.

## 7. Requesting Data Deletion

**All users, in every country, can ask us to delete their data at any time. It is free, and you do not have to give a reason.**

### Data on your headset

You can delete it yourself, immediately: **Settings → Reset All Progress** deletes your scores, coins and unlocked skins, and **uninstalling LumenVision** permanently deletes all of the app's data from your headset. Android's backup of the save file is managed in your Google account (Google Drive › Backups).

### Any other data

Email **davinci.dalhi@gmail.com** with the subject **"Data deletion request"**, from any email address, and tell us which data you want deleted. Within **30 days** we delete all data we hold about you, including any emails you have sent us, guide you through deleting the data stored on your headset and in your Google account's backup, and confirm the deletion to you by email.

### Data held by Google

Your Google account, your purchase of LumenVision and Android backups are managed by Google. You can view, download or delete them with Google's own tools ([myaccount.google.com](https://myaccount.google.com/)).
